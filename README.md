# Forex & Gold Trading — Complete Psychology Guide & 48-Step Mastery Checklist

**Instructor / Mentor:** D.M. Mahesh Dissanayaka (Lecturer in ICT)

An interactive, client-side web application designed for students and aspiring traders to learn institutional trading with professional discipline.

---

## 🎯 What's Inside

### 🧠 Part 1: Trading Psychology & Market Reality (8 Core Chapters)
1. **What Trading Really Is:** Why 95% fail & how institutional algorithms operate vs. sniper trading.
2. **The Biology of Trading:** How Dopamine (Greed) and the Amygdala (Fear & Anger) trigger bad decisions.
3. **The 6 Deadly Traps:** Revenge Trading, Overtrading, FOMO, Moving/Removing Stop Losses, Over-leveraging, and Strategy Hopping.
4. **Staying Safe:** Why Binary Options (IQ Option, Binomo, Quotex) and Telegram Signal Groups must be avoided.
5. **Redefining Losses:** Viewing Stop Losses as business operating expenses (understanding positive expectancy).
6. **The 5 Sniper Discipline Rules:** One & Done, No-Trade Box patience, 30m Candle Body Closes, Break-Even habits, and micro lot sizing.
7. **Life Discipline = Trading Discipline:** Balancing academic/vocational studies with trading.
8. **The 3-Month Roadmap:** Free Demo mastery to funded prop firm accounts.

### 📈 Part 2: 40-Step Technical & Practical Roadmap
- **Stage 01:** Accounts & Setup (TradingView & MetaTrader 5).
- **Stage 02:** Forex Basics & Candlestick Anatomy (Open, High, Low, Close, Body vs. Wick).
- **Stage 03:** Timeframes, Market Structure & Support/Resistance Zones.
- **Stage 04:** Smart Money Concepts (BOS, CHoCH, Order Blocks & FVG on EURUSD).
- **Stage 05:** Gold (XAUUSD) Market Fluidity & Clean Traffic Strategy (Raja Banks methodology).
- **Stage 06:** Risk Management, Trade Journaling & 5 Practical Submission Tasks.
